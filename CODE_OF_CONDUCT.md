# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CivicBridge AI a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Examples of positive behavior**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for citizens who use the product

**Unacceptable behavior**
- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sharing secrets, API keys, or credentials in issues/PRs
- Spam or malicious contributions

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive behavior may be reported to the maintainer via GitHub: [@Snigdha-0210](https://github.com/Snigdha-0210). The maintainer will review and take appropriate action.
